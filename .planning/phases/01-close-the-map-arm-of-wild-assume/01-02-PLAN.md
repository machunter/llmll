<objective>
Close the second seam and pin the over-breadth surface. Plan 01-01 proved the discriminant on the
return path; this plan covers the argument path, which is the live one, and adds the four controls
that say what the discriminant must NOT do.

Purpose: a guard is only as good as the set of cases that show it discriminating. Four of the six
fixtures here assert acceptance, not rejection. Without them the phase has shown only that
something fires, not that it fires on the right class.

Output: six new hspec examples (SA-8, SA-10, SA-11, SA-12, SA-13, SA-15) in the map-arm describe
block, and a measured answer to research open question 1 about alias expansion.
</objective>
