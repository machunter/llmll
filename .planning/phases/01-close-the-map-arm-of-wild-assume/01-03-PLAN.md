<objective>
Make the rejection say something true, measure the corpus and the suite against the plan 01-01
baseline, and settle the sidecar epoch question by evidence rather than by the reasoning the
research doc marked as assumed.

Purpose: the widen lands a rejection whose existing message describes a bytes fact. For a
`map[k,bool]` the asserted fact is a per-key value range, not a length, so shipping the widen
without touching the message ships a diagnostic that misdescribes what it refused. The corpus and
epoch work is the evidence half of ROADMAP criteria 4 and 5.

Output: `wildAssumeFactNoun` and SA-16, a recorded corpus comparison and example-count delta, and a
recorded decision on `checkerSoundnessVersion` with the evidence behind it.
</objective>
