## Roadmap criterion 1: correction of record

ROADMAP.md success criterion 1 reads as though the `(map-empty)` over-breadth fixture SA-6 must be
written in this phase. It must not be. SA-6 is already committed and already green at
`compiler/test/Spec.hs:2093-2096`, written ahead of need during the SAFE-ARG stage-1 work. Criterion
1 is therefore satisfied by confirming SA-6 before the widen and re-running it after, not by
authoring it. A diff that re-adds an `it "SA-6 ..."` block, or that moves the existing one, is the
warning sign the correction was missed.

There is a second, sharper point the roadmap wording hides, found by reading the fixture at HEAD:
**SA-6 does not exercise the widened clause at all.** SA-6 checks
`(def-shell m [k: int] -> map[int int] (map-empty))`. The expected type is `map[int,int]`, whose
value component is not bool, so `assumesFact` returns False for it both before and after this
phase. The `(map-empty)` position the widen actually puts at risk is a `map[int bool]` position,
which no committed fixture covers. SA-14 in Task 2 is that fixture. Both are required: SA-6 for
criterion 1 as written, SA-14 for the hazard criterion 1 was aiming at.
